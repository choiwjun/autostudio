## **Length Of YouTube Shorts**

Creators can make a YouTube Short of 3 minutes, depending on the content requirement. This section provides further insights into the length of YouTube Shorts.

### **How Long Is A YouTube Short?**

The maximum length of a YouTube Short is no longer 60 seconds. As of October 15, 2024, YouTube increased the maximum length of Shorts to 3 minutes (180 seconds). This was a highly requested feature from creators.

## **YouTube Shorts User Demographics**

The audience for YouTube Shorts skews younger, with the largest share falling between 18–34 years old. While older age groups are also active, Gen Z and Millennials make up a strong core of its users.

Although many assume that younger audiences primarily use TikTok or Instagram Reels, research shows that YouTube Shorts holds a significant position in the short-form video market across all age groups.

In fact, in some studies, it outperforms its competitors in both engagement and usage among younger viewers, fueled by YouTube’s massive global reach.

Source: [Zebracat.ai](http://zebracat.ai/), [AdamConnell.me](http://adamconnell.me/), AWISEE

## **YouTube Shorts Creator Earnings**

How much do creators earn from YouTube Shorts?

The answer may again vary according to the niche and target audience. Also, sponsored ads and the number of views affect the earnings of YouTube creators.

Let’s uncover the details about creator earnings through YouTube Shorts.

### **YouTube Shorts Creators Can Earn Between $0.01 – $0.06 Per 1,000 Views**

This amounts to around $10 to $60 for every million views.

That is far lower than the amount YouTube pays for long-form video content. Creators get paid between $1.61 and $29.30 per 1000 views for the long-form content on the platform.

![YouTube Shorts Creators Earning](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%201067'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

However, the Shorts get larger views compared to the long-form content.

**Source:** [Riverside](https://riverside.fm/blog/youtube-shorts-monetization).

### **How Many Views Are There on YouTube Shorts to Make $100?**

At a more realistic RPM of about $0.03, a creator would need roughly 3.3 million views to make $100. Even with a higher RPM of $0.05, it would still take around 2 million views to reach the same amount. Therefore, the claim that 100,000 views can generate $100 is exaggerated by 20 to 30 times.

**Source:** AIR Media
