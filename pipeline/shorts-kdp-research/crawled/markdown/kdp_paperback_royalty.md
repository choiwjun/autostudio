### Royalty calculation example

Royalty rates can be either 50% or 60%, depending on list price and marketplace.

- US Dollar VAT-exclusive List Price = $9.99
- Your book is a 300-page black ink paperback sold on the US marketplace
- Your Royalty per sale to a customer from Amazon.com is: (0.60 x $9.99) - $4.60 = $1.39

- US Dollar VAT-exclusive List Price = $9.98
- Your book is a 300-page black ink paperback sold on the US marketplace
- Your Royalty per sale to a customer from Amazon.com is: (0.50 x $9.98) - $4.60 = $0.39.

- (Royalty Rate x List Price) – Printing Costs = Royalty
- Applicable Printing Cost calculation: $1.00 (Fixed Cost) + (300 (Page Count) \* $0.012 (Per Page Cost)) = $4.60 (Printing Cost)

For more information about how royalty rates are impacted by list price and marketplace, please review our help pages [Paperback Royalty here](https://kdp.amazon.com/help?topicId=G201834330) and [Hardcover Royalty here](https://kdp.amazon.com/help?topicId=G77F3WPD3KQLJTFS).

### When we pay you

You will be paid Royalties due on paperback and hardcover book sales every month (1) approximately 60 days following the end of the calendar month during which the sales were made through standard Amazon distribution channels and (2) approximately 90 days after the end of the calendar month during which the sales were made through Expanded Distribution channels.

### Setting your list price

You will provide us with a list price (excluding tax) for each print book. This means VAT (Value Added Tax in Europe), GST (Goods and Services Tax in Australia) and JCT (Consumption Tax in Japan) should not be included in your list price. We will add the applicable taxes to the list price excluding VAT after the book is published depending on the customer's country or region and local VAT laws. The List Price for the corresponding digital and print editions of your book may be different, but must each comply with any pricing requirements applicable to them.

Learn more about [How EU Taxes Affect Print Books List Price.](https://kdp.amazon.com/help?topicId=GPQL5W3J6WNRCZTV)

### Other pricing terms

i. Minimum and Maximum List Prices: Print Books must have list prices within the minimum and maximum List Price range:

- The minimum List Price for your Print Book must be equal to its Printing Cost / 50% or 60% depending on list price. Find out more about Printing Costs by reading [Section C](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G8BKPU9AGVZSF9QF#sectionc) above.

- The maximum List Price for your Print Book is 250 USD (Amazon.com), 350 CAD (Amazon.ca), 250 EUR (Amazon.de, Amazon.es, Amazon.fr, Amazon.it, and Amazon.nl), 250 GBP (Amazon.co.uk), 350 AUD (Amazon.com.au), 30,000 JPY (Amazon.co.jp), 1,200 PLN (Amazon.pl), or 2,500 SEK (Amazon.se).


ii. Price Matching Determinations: If you notify us, by contacting KDP Customer Support, that you believe we have made an error in price-matching your Print Book, we will review the issue and make best efforts to correct the error, if any, going forward as soon as practicable.
