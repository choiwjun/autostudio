## What Works on YouTube Shorts?

YouTube Shorts performance is driven by retention, length, consistency, and topic choice. Key data points:

- Based on a study of **30M videos**, pranks, challenges, and “oddly satisfying” content receive the most views ( [_Source_](https://www.tubebuddy.com/blog/10-youtube-shorts-niches/)).
- **50-60s Shorts** perform best, achieving a **76%** watch-through rate ( [_Source_](https://awisee.com/blog/youtube-shorts-statistics/)).
- **Trending audio** boosts reach; using it in the first **5 seconds** increases reach by **21%**( [_Source_](https://awisee.com/blog/youtube-shorts-statistics/)).
- Upload volume matters as well: average creators publish **7 Shorts/month**, and top performers publish **18-22 Shorts/month** ( [_Source_](https://resourcera.com/data/social/youtube-shorts-statistics)).
- Consistency in posting YouTube Shorts helps drive growth: **6 months** of regular posting leads to a **+44% increase** in channel growth ( [_Source_](https://www.affiliatebooster.com/youtube-shorts-statistics)).
- **Viewed vs. Swiped Away (VVSA)** is a key metric: under **60%** means weak performance, **70-90%** signals strong results ( [_Source_](https://medium.com/@antoinelacombled/cracking-the-youtube-shorts-algorithm-a-study-of-3-3-billion-views-4711fdf7931b)).
- **Topic affects revenue, with** finance Shorts earning **10× more Revenue Per Mille (RPM)** than comedy or lifestyle ( [_Source_](https://influencermarketinghub.com/youtube-shorts-rpm/)).
