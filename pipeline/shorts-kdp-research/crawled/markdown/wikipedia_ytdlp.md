**youtube-dl** is a [free and open-source software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_and_open-source_software "Free and open-source software") tool for [downloading](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Download "Download") video and audio from [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube "YouTube")[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-3) and over 1,000 other video hosting websites.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-4) It supports diverse file formats, quality settings, and platforms including [Microsoft Windows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windows "Windows"), [macOS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacOS "MacOS"), and [Linux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linux "Linux"). It is released under the [Unlicense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unlicense "Unlicense") [software license](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_license "Software license").[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-5)

As of September 2021, youtube-dl is one of the most starred projects on [GitHub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GitHub "GitHub"), with over 100,000 stars.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-:12-6) According to libraries.io, 308 other packages and 1,430 repositories depend on it.[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-7) Numerous [forks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fork_(software_development) "Fork (software development)") of the project exist, among which the community-maintained fork [**yt-dlp**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#yt-dlp_fork) is notable for active development and enhanced functionality. yt-dlp builds upon youtube-dl's core features by offering improvements such as faster [multi-threaded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-threaded "Multi-threaded") downloads, more advanced format selection and subtitle embedding support. Due to its active maintenance and expanded capabilities, yt-dlp has been widely adopted and has replaced youtube-dl in several major Linux distributions, notably beginning with [Ubuntu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ubuntu "Ubuntu") 22.04.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-8)

Both tools have faced legal challenges, including takedown notices under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA), as well as controversies surrounding the downloading of copyrighted content.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-:12-6) Nonetheless, they remain important software for users requiring reliable media downloads for purposes including education, archiving, journalism, and [fair use](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fair_use "Fair use").

## History

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Youtube-dl&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: History")\]

Ricardo Garcia created youtube-dl in 2006.[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-9) Initially, only YouTube was supported, but as the project grew, it began supporting other video-sharing websites.[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-10)

Garcia stepped down as maintainer in 2011 and was replaced by Philipp Hagemeister,[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-11) who later stepped down and was replaced by dstftw.[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-12) In 2021, dstftw stepped down and was replaced by dirkf.[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-13)

In 2021, some community members released a [fork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fork_(software_development) "Fork (software development)") of youtube-dl, named youtube-dlc (for "community"). By January 2021, the effort was continuing as yt-dlp.[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-14) yt-dlp was included in [Ubuntu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ubuntu "Ubuntu") as of the 22.04 release.[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-15) youtube-dl was removed from Debian 12.0 and replaced with an empty package depending on yt-dlp.[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-16)

In August 2023, German company Uberspace was forced to take down a web domain which they hosted at their premises for the original youtube-dl project, citing a regional German court order issued from Landgericht Hamburg which appeared to ban the mere hosting of information and GitHub developer links related to the cracking of (non-cryptographic) "rolling ciphers."[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-17) The [GitHub subdomain webpage](https://ytdl-org.github.io/youtube-dl/) remains in place.

### RIAA takedown request

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Youtube-dl&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: RIAA takedown request")\]

On October 23, 2020, the [Recording Industry Association of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recording_Industry_Association_of_America "Recording Industry Association of America") (RIAA) issued a takedown notice to [GitHub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GitHub "GitHub") under the [Digital Millennium Copyright Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Millennium_Copyright_Act "Digital Millennium Copyright Act") (DMCA), requesting the removal of youtube-dl and 17 public [forks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_fork "Software fork") of the project. The RIAA request argued that youtube-dl violates the Section 1201 [anti-circumvention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-circumvention "Anti-circumvention") provisions of the DMCA, and provisions of [German copyright law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_copyright_law "German copyright law"), since it circumvents a "rolling cipher" used by YouTube to generate the [URL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/URL "URL") for the video file itself (which the RIAA has considered to be an effective [technical protection measure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_protection_measure "Technical protection measure"), since it is "intended to inhibit direct access to the underlying YouTube video files, thereby preventing or [inhibiting the downloading](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stream_ripping "Stream ripping"), copying, or distribution of the video files"),[\[18\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-18)[\[19\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-19)[\[20\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-20) and that its documentation expressly encouraged its use with copyrighted media by listing [music videos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_video "Music video") by RIAA-represented artists as examples. GitHub initially complied with the request.[\[21\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-:0-21)[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-:12-6)[\[22\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-22)

Users criticized the takedown, noting the legitimate uses for the application, including downloading video content released under [open licensing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_content "Open content") schemes or to create derivative works falling under [fair use](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fair_use "Fair use"), or other uses such as [journalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journalism "Journalism"), archival and [law enforcement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_enforcement "Law enforcement").[\[23\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-23)[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-:12-6)[\[24\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-24) Public attention to the takedown resulted in a [Streisand effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Streisand_effect "Streisand effect") reminiscent to that of the [DeCSS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeCSS "DeCSS") takedown. Users reposted the software's source code across the internet in multiple formats. For example, users posted images on [Twitter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twitter "Twitter") containing the whole youtube-dl source code encoded in different colors on each pixel.[\[25\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-:2-25) GitHub users also filed pull requests to GitHub's own repository of DMCA takedown notices that included youtube-dl source code.[\[25\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-:2-25)[\[26\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-26)

On November 16, 2020, the repository was reinstated, after the [Electronic Frontier Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Frontier_Foundation "Electronic Frontier Foundation") sent GitHub a letter cautioning that its removal might set a precedent for other copyright holders to misuse the [notice-and-takedown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notice_and_take_down "Notice and take down") process to remove software tools from the Internet based only on the argument that those tools _could_ be used for copyright infringement.[\[27\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-eff.org/2020/11/github-reinstates-youtube-dl-27) Furthermore, the EFF letter asserted that the software was not operating as a "circumvention device", breaching DRM on the video stream, as the stream itself was not encrypted.[\[27\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-eff.org/2020/11/github-reinstates-youtube-dl-27) GitHub also announced that future takedown claims under Section 1201 would be manually scrutinized on a case-by-case basis by legal and technical experts.[\[28\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-28)[\[29\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-29) In response to the saga, the platform launched a fund for providing expert support in writing [counter-notifications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notice_and_take_down "Notice and take down") when contested software was removed, in the hope that this would support fairer outcomes without adding too much friction to the platform's [content moderation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Content_moderation "Content moderation") processes.[\[30\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-30)

In response to development stagnation in youtube-dl, a [community fork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Community_fork "Community fork") named **yt-dlp** emerged, enhancing core features and expanding site support. yt-dlp provides faster downloads, improved format selection, and enhanced extraction logic.[\[33\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-archwiki-33)[\[34\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-yt-dlpgithub-34) The fork is actively maintained, has gained significant adoption, and was included in major Linux distributions such as [Ubuntu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ubuntu "Ubuntu") starting from version 22.04.[\[33\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youtube-dl#cite_note-archwiki-33)
