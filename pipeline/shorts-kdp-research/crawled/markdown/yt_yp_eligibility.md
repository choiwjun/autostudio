# YouTube Partner Program overview & eligibility

![](https://storage.googleapis.com/support-kms-prod/KsoV5vyv0ECEFfPB2kk0980HK2nW6yn7NfwT)

Starting February 1, 2027, we are introducing [updates to the YouTube Partner Program (YPP)](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009). To continue fully monetizing your content, review and accept the updated terms in YouTube Studio by January 31, 2027.

The YouTube Partner Program (YPP) gives creators greater access to YouTube resources and monetization features, and access to our [Creator Support](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3545535) teams. It also allows revenue sharing from ads being served on your content. Learn more about the features, eligibility criteria, and application details in this article.

## What you need to join

1. Follow the [YouTube channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392).


   - These are a collection of policies and guidelines that allow you to monetize on YouTube, and compliance with them is required when you accept a partner agreement with YouTube.
2. Live in a country/region [where the YouTube Partner Program is available](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7101720).
3. Have no active [Community Guidelines strikes](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2802032) on your channel.
4. Make sure [2-Step Verification](https://myaccount.google.com/signinoptions/two-step-verification/enroll-welcome) is turned on for your Google Account.
5. Have [advanced features](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9890437) access on YouTube.
6. Have one active [AdSense for YouTube account](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11602441) linked to your channel, or be ready to set one up in YouTube Studio if you don’t already have one (only create a new AdSense for YouTube account in YouTube Studio— [learn more](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9914702)).

## How you can become eligibleval

Once you understand what you need to join, your channel can become eligible for YPP with either Shorts or long-form video. If you’d like us to notify you when you’re eligible, select **Get notified** in the Earn area of YouTube Studio. You’ll get an email once you’ve met either of the below eligibility thresholds.

1\. Get 1,000 subscribers with 4,000 [qualified watch hours](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851#WHexplain) in the last 12 months, or

2\. Get 1,000 subscribers with 10 million [qualified Shorts views](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851#WHexplain) in the last 90 days.

Keep in mind that any qualified watch hours from Shorts views in the Shorts Feed won’t count towards the 4,000 qualified watch hours threshold.

### More on eligibility thresholds

These thresholds help us make a more informed decision about whether your channel meets our policies and guidelines. Once you apply, your channel will go through a standard review process to see whether your channel meets our policies and guidelines. If it meets our policies and guidelines, we’ll accept your channel into YPP. Keep in mind we continuously check channels in YPP to make sure they continue to meet our policies and guidelines over time.

## How we review your application

Once you accept YPP terms and link an active AdSense for YouTube account, your channel will automatically be put in a review queue. Our automated systems and human reviewers will review your channel as a whole to make sure your channel follows all of our policies and guidelines. Check back in the **Earn** section of YouTube Studio anytime to see the status of your application.

We’ll get back to you with a decision once your channel is reviewed ( **typically in about 1 month**).

Keep in mind delays are possible due to higher-than-usual application volumes, system issues, or resource limitations. All YPP applications are serviced in the order they’re received by us. Sometimes channels require multiple reviews, especially when several reviewers disagree on your channel’s suitability for YPP. This may increase the time required for a decision to be made.

If your first application wasn’t successful, don’t worry - you can appeal the decision within 21 days or keep uploading original content and you’ll be able to re-apply after a 30-day period. If this isn’t your first application to be rejected, or you’ve previously re-applied, you can try again after a 90-day period. Our reviewers likely found that a significant portion of your channel doesn’t currently follow our policies and guidelines, so be sure to review those against your channel’s overall content and adjust your channel before re-applying. Learn more about [steps you can take](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9235730) to strengthen your application for next time.

As the YouTube Partner Program continues to grow, it's important to maintain a healthy, active ecosystem of channels. ## What if I don’t meet the program threshold?

If you don’t meet the requirements yet, keep working to make original content and build your audience. Here are some resources that can help you grow your channel:

- Learn solutions from other YouTube users in the [YouTube Help Forum](https://support.google.com/youtube/community/).
- Discover quick-and-easy tips on how to grow and build your channel in our [Creator Tips](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11616410) hub.
- Go to [youtube.com/creators](https://www.youtube.com/yt/creators/) to learn, grow, and be connected to programs, resources, and events.
- Check out our [YouTube Help channel](https://www.youtube.com/user/YouTubeHelp) and [YouTube Creators channel](https://www.youtube.com/user/partnersupport) to watch tutorials, troubleshooting, and tip videos from the YouTube Help team.

If you have active [Community Guidelines strikes](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2802032) on your channel, you can apply after your strikes expire. You can also apply after you've successfully [appealed](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/185111) to have your Community Guidelines strikes removed. Current members of the program won't be removed when receiving Community Guidelines strikes.

What do “qualified watch hours” and "qualified Shorts views" mean?

### Qualified watch hours

What counts as a qualified watch hour:

- Qualified watch hours gained from long-form videos you’ve set public

Watch hours gained through the following types of video don’t count towards YPP thresholds:

- Private videos
- Unlisted videos
- Deleted videos
- Ad campaigns
- YouTube Shorts
- Livestreams that are unlisted, deleted, or not converted to VOD (video on demand)

### Qualified Shorts views

What counts as a qualified Short view:

- Qualified views from Shorts you’ve set public that appear in the Shorts Feed

Shorts views gained through the following types of videos don’t count towards YPP thresholds:

- Private Shorts videos
- Unlisted Shorts videos
- Deleted Shorts videos
- Ad campaigns
- Image Posts appearing in the Shorts Feed

Keep in mind that any qualified watch hours from Shorts views in the Shorts Feed don’t count towards the 4,000 qualified watch hours threshold. For info about Shorts view eligibility with regards to how we calculate payments, see our [YouTube Shorts monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220#ineligible-views).

## If I meet the threshold, do I automatically get into YPP?

No. Every channel that meets the threshold will go through a standard review process. Our team will review your channel as a whole to check whether your channel meets our [YouTube channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392). Your channel also must not have any active [Community Guidelines strikes](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2802032) in order to apply. Channels that follow our policies and guidelines can monetize.

What happens if my counts drop below the threshold after I apply?

We’ll send your channel for review once you meet our qualified watch hours and subscriber threshold. So it doesn’t matter if your subscriber or watch hour counts drop below the threshold while waiting for review. If you’ve met the threshold and applied for YPP, we’ll still review your channel for YPP suitability.

[Changes to your video privacy settings](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/157177) or [deletion of video](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/55770) might impact the status of your application. You can check the status of your application at any time in the Earn section of YouTube Studio.

A channel needs to complete all the YPP signup steps (which currently include signing the contract and associating an AdSense for YouTube account) before they will be reviewed.

If a channel is inactive and has not uploaded or posted posts for 6 months or more, YouTube reserves the right to remove its monetization.

Channels will lose monetization when they violate any of the [YouTube channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392). This loss of monetization will happen regardless of their watch hours and subscriber count.

I’m no longer in YPP (or I was never in the program) and I’m seeing ads on my videos. Am I earning revenue from those ads?

According to our Terms of Service, YouTube retains the right to serve ads on all content on the platform. If you do not currently meet the higher eligibility criteria of YPP you may still see ads being served on your content. In this case, you are not yet eligible to receive a share of the revenue.

Once you qualify for the higher eligibility criteria for YPP, you will be eligible to receive a share of the revenue from ads served on your content.
